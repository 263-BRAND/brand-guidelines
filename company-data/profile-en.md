# Net263 Ltd. Company Profile

## Company Profile

Net263 Ltd. (263 Group, stock code: 002467.SZ) was founded in 1997 and listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in September 2010. As a **global digital intelligence communications service provider**, 263 Group is committed to building ubiquitous connectivity to enhance communication experience and organizational efficiency.

Over 29 years of deep engagement in the industry, 263 Group leverages its three core capabilities — **Global Network Services**, **Smart Services**, and **AI-Powered Digital Content** — as pillars to build a comprehensive, all-scenario digital and intelligent connectivity system. Its business operations span numerous countries and regions, including China, North America, Latin America, the Middle East, Australia, Singapore, Japan, and South Korea, and it has served a cumulative total of **150,000 corporate clients and tens of millions of individual users** worldwide.

## Three Core Capabilities

### Global Network Services

263 Group possesses comprehensive capabilities in global network infrastructure. Supported by four core segments — Data Centers, Global Direct Connectivity, International Private Network, and Mobile Communications — 263 Group provides stable, secure, and efficient connectivity services to customers worldwide, laying a solid foundation for its digital intelligence communications services portfolio.

### Smart Services

263 Group deeply integrates communication products — such as voice calls, instant messaging, live video streaming, and email services — with AI technology. It focuses on developing a series of products, including Smart Customer Service, Smart Live Streaming, Smart Email, and Smart Devices, to continuously provide efficient smart communications experiences for government agencies, businesses, and home users.

### AI-Powered Digital Content

With "data-driven + intelligent agent technology" at its core, 263 Group has built a full-chain digital content system covering Digital Employees, Intelligent Agent Platforms, Knowledge Management, and Content Quality Inspection. This system helps enterprises achieve a dual leap forward in operational efficiency and service experience, while driving the innovative development of digital intelligence services.

## Mission

Guided by its corporate mission of **"Connect & Empower,"** 263 Group continues to expand the scale of its connections, enhance the quality of its connection services, and strengthen its connection applications, all to jointly drive industrial and social progress, share global ecosystem resources, and co-create an open, intelligent era.
